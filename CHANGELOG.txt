EB Games Auto Checkout — Stable Core v1.8.0

This is a low-risk backend/orchestration refactor based on the v1.7.1 baseline.

Changes:
- Preserves the tested product-page and sequential cart-refresh scripts unchanged.
- Guards against starting overlapping product-tab runs from the popup.
- Centralises result queuing and makes background result recording idempotent.
- Closes a confirmed product tab only when its result belongs to the active run.
- Makes popup start-state handling more explicit.

Not changed: checkout/payment automation, cart quantity event semantics, product click semantics, refresh wait timings.

This build has not been live-tested against EB Games. Verify every visible quantity and cart total manually.
